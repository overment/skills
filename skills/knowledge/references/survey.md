# Survey

Open this when starting a base, or when an existing one no longer fits its use. A base exists to answer questions, so list the questions before any folder (jobs to be done).

## Look first

- **Existing base:** sample 20 to 30 notes across folders and ages. Note the conventions people follow and the ones they ignore; both are evidence.
- **New base:** use the conversation, the material to bring in, and the tools already in use.

## Establish

1. **Questions:** five to ten, in the person's words, such as "why did we choose X?" or "what do I know about Y?". If they can't name any, ask what they last failed to find. The finding test uses these.
2. **Readers and writers:** people, agents or both, and who arrives without context.
3. **Inflow:** what arrives, from where, how often and in what state. Pick ten real or likely items for the filing test.
4. **Volume:** how many notes now, in a year and in three, and whether they come by hand or from automated feeds. Past a few thousand, read [scale](scale.md) before designing.
5. **Decay:** what goes stale (prices, people, procedures) and what doesn't (history, principles).
6. **Medium and constraints:** where the base lives, how agents reach it, and what can't change, such as privacy, sync or team habits.

Summarize the answers, with the questions verbatim. The summary opens the base's [skill](instructions.md).
